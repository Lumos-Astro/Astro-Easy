// UCs and Deadlines
📜

Thesis Project

A research plan for your whole thesis: the state of the art, your methods, your goals and how you plan to reach them.

  • Written proposal + presentation with discussion at the end of the academic year
  • Max. 20 pages, using the mandatory LaTeX template (you can adapt it)
  • Must include a timeline — later annual reports compare against it
  • Submitted with declarations from your supervisor(s) and research unit
  • The title can still be changed later if needed
Topic & adviser by 15 Nov · 15 Dec if no pre-defined supervisor
🔬

Seminar

A closed, short research task with a pre-defined objective — about 2–3 months of full-time work. Good preparation for your thesis project.

  • Report + presentation with discussion
  • Use the “Thesis Project” LaTeX template, adapting the sections (max. 20 pages)
  • Templates will be provided
Work & adviser by 15 Nov
🎒

Formação Complementar

A unit that corresponds to a workshop or class you attend, such as a school, course or training event.

  • Discuss and agree with your supervisor what counts
Flexible — plan it with your supervisor
